End-to-end delivery

  • Site survey and load assessment before any quote

02

DC fast-charging focus

Power sized to how your site is used.

We recommend 60, 120 or 240 kW DC charging from how long vehicles stay, how many charge at once and the supply your site has, not from the biggest number on a brochure.

Explore Chargers
  • 60 kW for destinations and fleet bays with longer stops
  • 120 kW for busy commercial sites and highway stops
  • 240 kW for high-throughput corridors and depots
  • CCS2 connectors, used by most new electric cars in India
  • Configuration confirmed after a site assessment
03

Standards-based hardware

Open protocols. No lock-in.

Our chargers are specified to connect over OCPP, the open protocol most charge-point management platforms use, so you can choose or change your software later without replacing hardware.

Talk to an Expert
  • OCPP 1.6J backend connectivity, confirmed per model
  • Works with Envizon or third-party management software
  • CCS2 connector to IEC 62196-3
  • Session and status data available to your operator
  • Hardware documentation shared at handover
04

Local installation & support

Teams on the ground when it matters.

Installation and service are handled by on-ground teams who know your site, with planned maintenance and a clear way to raise and track faults once you are live.
