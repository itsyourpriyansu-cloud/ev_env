# Envizon EV: Image & Video Art Direction

**For:** the photographer, videographer or designer producing imagery for the Envizon EV homepage.
**Goal:** imagery that feels specific, real and premium, not stock-generic or AI-made.

Every asset in this brief replaces a slot that already exists on the site. If you deliver the exact file names and sizes listed, it drops straight in with no layout change.

---

## 1. The look: "Real infrastructure, quietly confident"

Document EV charging as it actually exists in Hyderabad, the way an architecture photographer would: calm, precise, lived-in. It should look like a real place you could drive to tomorrow, not a vision of the future.

| Pillar | What it means on camera |
|---|---|
| **Documentary, not staged** | Real sites, real hardware, real Hyderabad context: basement parking pillars, society gates, ORR fuel plazas, depot sheds. People are incidental and mid-task, never smiling at camera. |
| **Precise & architectural** | Strong verticals, level horizons, a 35–50 mm feel. Leave deliberate negative space for the website's text. The hardware is the hero, shot with the care of a product photo. |
| **Calm light** | Overcast daylight, early morning, blue hour, or clean basement fluorescents balanced to neutral. No harsh noon sun, no neon. |
| **Brand-true colour** | Green appears only where it really exists: the charger's light strip, a status LED, painted EV bays, trees. Nothing green is added in grading. |
| **Honest texture** | Dust on bollards, tyre marks, coiled cables, scuffed paint, real signage and parking numbers. Small imperfections are what make an image believable. |

### Colour grade (all stills and video)
- **White balance:** 5200–5600 K, neutral to slightly warm. Never teal-and-orange.
- **Shadows:** lift slightly toward a deep green-black (≈ `#0E1C18`), never crushed to pure black.
- **Highlights:** clean, not clipped.
- **Saturation:** −10 to −15 %. Keep greens close to the brand range (`#00A650` → `#71BF45`); never fluorescent.
- **Grain:** a subtle film-like grain (2–4 %) on every final export. The website uses a fine noise texture, so grain helps photos sit naturally on the page.
- **Hero exposure:** the website darkens the hero video by 40 %, so grade it to hold detail in the shadows.

### Composition rules for every image
- Keep the **text-safe zones** (listed per slot below) calm: low contrast, no faces, no signage.
- **No dominant third-party branding:**
  - car badges, mall or fuel-station logos, or other charger makers' badges
  - angle away from them, or keep them small and soft
- **Number plates:** blur or crop out.
- **Connectors must be correct:**
  - **CCS2** for DC fast charging (60 / 120 / 240 kW)
  - **Type 2** for AC
- **Vehicles:** EVs that are genuinely common in India (compact SUVs, sedans, e-autos, fleet cabs), shot so badges aren't the focal point.

## 2. What to avoid (the "AI / generic" tells)

- **Visual effects:** glowing lightning bolts, neon light trails, holograms, blue sci-fi glow, "energy" particles.
- **Unreal scenes:** futuristic concept cars, empty spotless showrooms, perfectly symmetrical "render" scenes.
- **Stock clichés:** plug-in-hand close-ups with a smile, people looking at the camera, handshake shots.
- **Wrong place:** Western suburbs, snow, left-hand-drive cues, non-Indian plates or signage.
- **Generation artefacts:**
  - warped or unreadable text
  - extra or melted connectors, cables that go nowhere
  - plastic skin, odd hands
  - impossible reflections
  - over-smooth gradients
  - everything razor-sharp at once
- **Over-processing:** drone sunsets, HDR halos, heavy vignettes, added lens flares.
- **Fake screens:** a charger screen shows a plausible, minimal interface or is switched off.

## 3. Shot list by section

### 3.1 Hero background video: top of the page, full screen (highest priority)

| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| Where it shows | Full-bleed behind the headline: 1440×900 on laptops, 1920×900 on large screens, **portrait 390×900 on phones** |
| Deliver | `hero-1920.mp4` (16:9, 1920×1080) · `hero-960.mp4` (**separate vertical 9:16 cut**, 1080×1920) · `hero-poster.webp` (first frame, 1920×1080) |
| Format | 18–24 s **seamless loop**, 25 fps, H.264, no audio. Target ≤ 10 MB desktop, ≤ 3 MB mobile. |
| Camera movement | Slow slider or gimbal moves at walking pace or slower. 4–5 shots of 4–5 s each with soft cuts. No whip pans or fast cuts. **The last frame must match the first** so the loop is invisible. |
| Text-safe zones | Headlines sit across the **middle band (≈ 38–62 % of the height)**. Short descriptors and buttons sit across the **lower band (≈ 55–75 %)** over the full width. Keep those bands calm and mid-to-dark. Put visual interest in the upper half or in gentle moving texture. |
| Vertical cut | The same shots re-framed vertically, with the subject centred horizontally. |

**Shot sequence**

1. **Wide establishing.** A modern commercial basement or covered car park with a row of DC chargers under soft fluorescent light. An EV pulls in slowly.
2. **Medium.** A hand lifts a CCS2 gun from its holster. No face.
3. **Close.** The connector seats into the car's inlet, and the charger's status light turns green.
4. **Detail.** The charger screen and light strip glow softly. A coiled cable, dust on the floor marking.
5. **Exterior.** A dusk ORR or highway charging plaza with traffic moving at natural speed (no long-exposure streaks).
6. Return to the opening frame for the loop.

## 4. How to source it (in order of preference)

1. **Commission one shoot day in Hyderabad** at two or three real sites: a commercial basement car park, a residential society, and an ORR plaza or fleet depot. One day covers the hero video, the three product images and the banners. This is the only route that's genuinely non-generic.
2. **Licensed stock, as a stop-gap for the hero video only.** Choose India-specific clips with an editorial feel and re-grade them to §1. Never use stock for the product images.
3. **AI image generation: mood boards and pre-visualisation only.** It can't depict our real hardware, and it produces the tells in §2. If you use it for a mood board, feed it the §2 list as negative prompts and grade the result to §1. Nothing AI-generated goes on the live site.

## 5. Delivery checklist

- [ ] RAW files plus graded exports.
- [ ] Stills: sRGB WebP, quality ≈ 82, exact sizes and file names from §3.
- [ ] Video: H.264 MP4 (CRF 26–28, "faststart"), no audio, seamless loop, sizes within target.
- [ ] Every subject sits inside its safe area. Check each crop at 1440, 1920 and phone width.
- [ ] Headline readability is checked on at least three points of the hero loop (after the site's 40 % darkening).
- [ ] No dominant third-party logos; number plates blurred; connectors correct (CCS2 / Type 2).
- [ ] Model and property releases for any person or private site.
- [ ] Licence details supplied (source, owner, usage scope, expiry) for each asset.
